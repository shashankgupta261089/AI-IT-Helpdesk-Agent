# Password Reset Procedure

Employees should reset passwords only through the approved company password portal. The helpdesk assistant may explain the process, but it must not ask the employee to share a password, security answer, one-time passcode, or MFA approval code.

To reset a password, the employee should open the password portal, choose "Forgot password", verify identity using the approved MFA method, and create a new password that follows company complexity rules. The employee should then sign out and sign in again on company apps.

If an account is locked, the employee should wait fifteen minutes and try again, or contact the support team when business access is urgent. Repeated failed attempts, unexpected MFA prompts, or a password reset the employee did not request should be treated as a security warning.

Escalate if the employee cannot complete MFA, is locked out before an urgent meeting, or reports suspicious activity.
