# WiFi Troubleshooting Guide

Company WiFi may be affected by local device settings, weak signal, or a broader network outage. Ask the employee whether other nearby employees are also affected. If many people are affected in the same area, check service status and escalate if needed.

For a single-device issue, ask the employee to turn WiFi off and on, forget and rejoin the company network, and confirm they are using the correct company WiFi network. If the employee is remote, remind them that home WiFi is not managed by IT, but basic device troubleshooting may still help.

If the WiFi status is degraded, explain that there may be an active network issue and recommend using a wired connection or mobile hotspot if approved.

Escalate when a full office area is affected, business-critical work is blocked, or the employee still cannot connect after the standard steps.
