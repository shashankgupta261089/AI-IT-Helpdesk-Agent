# VPN Troubleshooting Guide

The company VPN should be used whenever employees need secure access to internal systems from outside the office network. Before escalating a VPN issue, ask the employee to confirm that their normal internet connection is working by opening a public website.

If the internet connection is healthy, close and reopen the VPN client. Restarting the VPN client clears many stale sessions. After reopening the client, reconnect using the employee's company username and approved multi-factor authentication method.

If the VPN repeatedly fails after valid MFA approval, ask the employee to restart the computer and try one more connection attempt. Employees should not approve unexpected MFA prompts. Unexpected MFA activity may indicate a security issue and should be escalated.

Escalate the issue when the employee has an urgent business need, cannot connect after these steps, sees repeated authentication failures, or reports suspicious MFA prompts.
